MoonshotMail

Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

MoonshotMail is a personal macOS email client. Its developer builds it for their own use with their own Google accounts, and it isn't distributed to anyone else. This policy describes how MoonshotMail handles data it receives from Google.

What MoonshotMail accesses

When you sign in with Google and grant access, MoonshotMail requests:

  • Gmail (gmail.modify): your messages, threads, labels, and drafts, so it can show, search, and organize your mail and send replies you write. This scope can't permanently delete mail, and MoonshotMail never asks for one that can.
  • Basic profile (openid, email, profile): your name and email address, to tell your accounts apart.

How it uses that data

  • To display your mail as conversations, search it, and organize it with Gmail labels.
  • To send email only when you explicitly choose to send it.
  • To generate optional summaries using Apple's on-device models. Your mail is never sent to an outside AI service.

Where it's stored

MoonshotMail runs entirely on your Mac and talks directly to Google's APIs, plus the sender-picture lookups described below. It keeps a local copy of mail metadata and content in a database on your Mac so it can work quickly and search offline, and it stores sign-in tokens in the macOS Keychain. There's no MoonshotMail server. The app has no analytics, telemetry, or advertising.

Sender pictures

To show a picture beside each sender, MoonshotMail looks in this order:

  • A photo or emoji you chose for that sender, kept only on your Mac.
  • The sender's photo in your Mac's Contacts. MoonshotMail reads Contacts only with your permission, on your Mac, and never changes or uploads them.
  • The sender's own website, for a company address. MoonshotMail requests the public home page of the sender's domain and the icon files it lists. The request names only the domain, never your address or anything from your mail.
  • Gravatar (gravatar.com), for a person. MoonshotMail sends a one-way SHA-256 hash of the sender's email address, which is how Gravatar looks pictures up.

Pictures, and "no picture found" results, are cached on your Mac, so each source is asked rarely. Logos and Gravatars aren't shown beside messages that fail Gmail's sender authentication. Nothing else from your mail is sent anywhere.

Sharing

MoonshotMail doesn't sell your Google data, and it doesn't share or transfer it to anyone, except for the sender-picture lookups above: a sender's domain goes to that domain's own website, and a hash of a sender's address goes to Gravatar. It doesn't use your data for advertising, and it doesn't use your data to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

MoonshotMail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Removing an account in MoonshotMail deletes its sign-in tokens from your Mac. Deleting the app's data removes the local copy of your mail. You can revoke MoonshotMail's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your mail itself stays in Gmail.
